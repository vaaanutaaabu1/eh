How does the plagiarism check work?

Distinctive phrases from your text are searched on the web, the pages that come back are read, and every sentence is compared with them. Sentences that share long runs of words with a page are marked and linked to that page.

Is it the same as Turnitin?

No. It checks the open web, so it finds copied and lightly reworded passages from public pages. It cannot see paywalled journals or other students' private submissions, which are in Turnitin's own database. A low score means nothing was found online, not a guarantee for your school's checker.
